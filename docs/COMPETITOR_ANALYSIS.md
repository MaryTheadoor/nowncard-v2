# NownCard — Competitive Analysis 2026

> Source: `Downloads/Digital Card Market Analysis.docx` (Kimi market research), reviewed 2026-08-09.
> Competitors mapped: 20+ across digital business cards and link-in-bio / landing pages.

---

## Key Findings

- **Price is NownCard's biggest advantage.** $19/yr Pro and $39/yr Business is roughly
  **1/4 to 1/9 the cost** of most competitors' paid tiers. Closest functional competitor on
  price is KADO at $48/yr; Wave is $84/yr, Blinq $88/yr, HiHello $72/yr, Popl $96/yr.
- **Market is growing fast.** ~$217–238M in 2026, expanding at 8.86–12.6% CAGR toward
  $331–680M by the early 2030s. **37% of businesses** have adopted digital cards (up from
  16% in 2020).
- **Positioning:** NownCard sits in the **high-value quadrant** — low annual cost with solid
  feature richness. Most competitors cluster at $72–180/yr.

## Critical Feature Gaps (parity analysis)

| Missing Feature | Why It Matters | Competitors Who Have It |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **Apple/Google Wallet Passes** | Standard expectation for digital cards | Wave, Blinq, HiHello, Popl, DBC, V1CE |
| **Lead Capture Forms** | Two-way contact exchange becoming table stakes | DBC (flagship), Wave, Popl, KADO |
| **SOC 2 Type II Compliance** | Required for enterprise/team sales | Wave, Blinq, HiHello, Popl, Uniqode |
| **CRM Integrations** | Unlocks the sales professional segment | KADO, Popl, Wave, Linq, HiHello |

## Strategic Recommendations

### Immediate wins (0–3 months)
1. **Add Wallet Passes** — the #1 feature gap; every major competitor offers it.
2. **Implement Lead Capture** — a simple form that captures visitor info and emails it to
   the card owner would match DBC's core value prop.
3. **Pursue SOC 2** — becoming a procurement requirement.

### Growth plays (3–6 months)
1. **Launch an NFC Tag Store** — sell pre-programmed NFC tags/cards. We already support
   NFC programming from Android Chrome.
2. **Team Admin Dashboard** — Business plan ($39/yr) needs centralized management to
   compete with HiHello Team ($5/user/mo) and Wave Teams.
3. **Custom Domains** — Pro/Business users get `card.yourdomain.com`.

### Positioning
- Own the **"Affordable Professional"** narrative:
  - vs. DBC: 77% cheaper · vs. Blinq: 78% cheaper · vs. Linktree Pro: 89% cheaper · vs. Linq: 94% cheaper
- **Double down on the schemorphic UI** ("it actually looks like a card") — no one else
  leads with this; in a sea of generic link-in-bio pages it's a genuine differentiator.
- **Target local businesses with the Online Menu feature** — restaurants, food trucks, and
  venues are an underserved vertical in the digital card space (feature shipped 2026-08-07).
